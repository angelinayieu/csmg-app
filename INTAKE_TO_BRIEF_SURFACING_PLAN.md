## 1. The principle

**Stop building features that hide in the drawer. Make every stage
appear in the chat with a content-rich body, and make every v3
field reach the brief.** The drawer becomes the *deep-expand
affordance*; the user lives in the chat + the brief.

Two design rules:
- **A. The chatbox is the trace.** Every autopilot stage emits a
  chat-style row with a 2-3 sentence body the user reads without
  expanding, plus an "open in [drawer | brief | view]" deep-link
  for the rare case they want depth.
- **B. The brief is the deliverable.** It surfaces every load-bearing
  field — including the v3 experience layer. Auto-fires at the end
  of autopilot; the user opens the chat, sees "Your strategy brief
  is ready: [link]", clicks once, reads the whole thing.

## 6. What NOT to do

- **Don't add a new event bus / SSE channel for chat narration.** The
  existing polled `sub_objective_decisions` rows + the `narration_*`
  metadata extension is sufficient and avoids the "wrong subsystem"
  pitfall called out in `NOTEBOOK_TIMELINE_PLAN.md` §6.
- **Don't migrate the action CHECK piecemeal.** ALWAYS re-assert the
  full superset (per `[[project_parallel_workstreams]]`).
- **Don't auto-register every novel `produces`/`consumes` token
  silently.** The auto-registration should be opt-in by the
  generator caller (the route handler), with a soft confidence
  threshold from `fuzzyMatchUnit`. Silent auto-register pollutes
  the registry with LLM hallucinations.
- **Don't build a "macro data flow" tab as a new top-level view.**
  Per `SYSTEMS_WIRING_MASTER_PLAN.md` §A: prototype lives in
  `preflight/data-lineage-preview`; wire it as the 4th Goal-card
  tab. Coordinate before mounting.
- **Don't move the Experience view out of the drawer.** It's deep
  detail that the brief will surface via `ExperienceBriefSection`
  for the user. Two surfaces over the same data (drawer + brief)
  is fine; making the drawer-only view the primary surface is
  the trap.

## 7. Build order (recommended)

Highest leverage first:

1. **§3.3** (one session) — `compile-agent-build-spec.ts`:
   `applyDependsOn` + `composeExperienceBriefSection` integration.
   The brief immediately gains v3 depth.
2. **§3.4** (one session) — brief render of the experience section.
   The user can OPEN the brief and see everything.
3. **§3.5 (d)** (one session) — autopilot auto-fires brief polish.
   The user gets the "ready" moment without clicking.
4. **§3.2** (parallel) — every route writes `narration_*` metadata.
   Cheap. Just merge composer output into existing `logDecision`
   calls.
5. **§3.1** (one session) — notebook reads `narration_*` keys and
   renders chat-style rows + deep-links. The user lives in the chat.
6. **§4** (one migration) — new actions. After §1-5 are live.
7. **§3.6** (one session) — collapse the intake gates. The user
   never has to click pre-autopilot.

Step 1 alone moves us from 45% → 75% brief coverage. Step 1+3+4
gives the user the full autopilot-to-brief trace. The rest is
polish on a working system.
